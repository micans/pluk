  Sections 12-14 (recipes, messages and reports, differences from pick) follow in step31;

  Outcome of the discussion

  All proposals agreed as written; BAM: (a), a small doc/reads.bam.

  Decisions:

   1. Content of section 10, in three parts:
      a. Reading SAM: --sam (implies -k -O12, @ lines skipped; columns 1-11 and 12 the
         optional fields), --sam-h (@ lines passed through), --sam/FILE (the reference
         fasta as dictionary SAMFA), BAM recognised and read as SAM, --threads; the @HD
         SO warning; the normal form (exterior D and I; --sam-nonf); the sequence fill
         for SEQ '*' (secondary records; --sam-seqmiss=skip|exit); NA in a paragraph,
         pointing to SAM-NA.md.
      b. The operator families, each with one or two invocations and links: positions and
         lengths (ref_posx .. qry_len), trails and matched parts, CIGAR parts
         (aln_*_cgr, cgsum cgmax cgcount), sequence parts (needs --sam/FILE), counts and
         edits (aln_nmatch aln_nedit, posinfo), patchiness, sam_get and sam_put (types,
         the escape hatch), isforward.
      c. Alignment display and its options: aln_all aln_allq aln_all_pack aln_ref
         aln_qry, the alignment symbols, --sam-aln-prefix --sam-aln-mrk --sam-qlt-sim;
         view options --sam-aln-context --sam-aln-xy --sam-narrow-xy (their order with
         respect to each other); --sam-ND-adjust (N that overlaps none of the given
         intervals becomes D; first of the rewrites); the region-bound tally --sam-rbt
         and sam_rbt.

   2. Content of section 11: the bio operators (rc forward todna dnatl dnagc fasta fastq
      fastQ strpair) by use: reverse complement and strand, translation and GC content,
      writing FASTA and FASTQ from SAM (a short recipe: unmapped reads to FASTQ), strpair.
      Mostly links; it is the shortest section.

   3. Example data: the existing transgene.fa, reads.sam, mixed.sam, align.sam as far as
      they go. New, small: a spliced SAM file (doc/spliced.sam: a reference with a short
      intron, a read with an N that overlaps a given interval and one that does not, for
      --sam-ND-adjust; a read with exterior D/I for the normal form; an @HD line). The
      existing files stay unchanged so that the examples of sections 1-9, 15 and the
      introduction keep their outputs.

   4. BAM in the examples: either (a) a small doc/reads.bam made from reads.sam with
      test/mkbam.py, so that 'pluk --sam < reads.bam' is a tested example; or (b) BAM
      described in prose only. Proposal: (a); the file is a few hundred bytes, binary.

   5. When section 10 is complete the Makefile runs optcheck.py --strict (decision 3 of
      step29). Proposal: in item 3, the item that writes the last SAM options.
