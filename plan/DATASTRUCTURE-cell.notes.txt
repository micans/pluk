
The basic unit in pluk will be called Scalar and initally have members that are similar
to the following:

  String: s
  i128: i
  double: f
  u32: notes

The first three members will likely need to be custom types, as they need to be able to represent NA (not available). The numeric
types also need to be able to represent NaN (not a number).
Discuss what common choices there are, how they affect the fast paths of computation, and what considerations
are specific to the niche that pluk operates in.

The last member 'notes' is to store anticipated debug, analytics and synchronisation information/signals, for example
to indicate what member was produced or must be produced.

Fields read from the input will be represented by a Scalar, their s pointing to a
stretch of the input line. Scalar.s will likely be immutable; either it is read from a field
or produced by a computation, but in both cases immutable.
Consider whether some string operations might be slightly more efficient/fast if re-using Scalar.s
from one of the operands.

Operators will consume zero, one or more Scalars from the stack and push a number of scalars that
is tied to the operator.
Some operators will purely manipulate the stack, e.g. xch (switches the two top-level Scalars).

Discuss.
