Item 1: section 13, messages and reports

  Drawn from the source: every eprintln! and note! site (main.rs opt.rs list.rs; note!
  in stages.rs filter.rs sam.rs samrows.rs dict.rs io.rs demux.rs compute.rs colspec.rs
  select.rs). The section: stdout data only; errors (exit 1; most before input is read,
  four kinds during the run, listed); warnings (when they occur); the end-of-run reports
  as a table (report, what it counts, section); -q (everything but errors).

  Found while surveying, listed in the section as they are and not changed (decision 1):
  - errors found while reading options and arguments are written without the 'pluk:'
    prefix ('--sam-nonf requires --sam or --sam-h', '[s::kg,nosuch]: unknown operator',
    'Unrecognised long option --bogus', the -/PAT regex error); errors found later have
    it;
  - warnings without the prefix: '-- You used X (a column handle) as a constant ..',
    '-- range expansion produced some indexes out of range', '-- Identical output column
    names exist' (spurious with -i, step29 item 3), 'Line N does not look like SAM', and
    the --demux tally;
  - pluk without column arguments writes its usage note and exits with status 0.
  Candidates for a small code step: give every message the prefix (the doc examples then
  need no 2>&1), and decide on the exit status without arguments.
