  Outcome of the discussion

  Decisions:
   0. Shape of a body section, as pick's manual: a few useful invocations first, then the
      details and the options, each with a tested example. Operators are not described
      again: the body names them and links to the reference (section 15), so every rule
      is written once. Options are described in the body, in the section where they
      belong (the reference has no options).

   1. Content per section (MANUAL.design.txt section 2):
      4  names, ':' lists, '+' regular expressions (whole-name, anchored), -k ranges
         (forms O+S-E*M); -A, -A<N>, --A=NAME, -x, -U; repeated output columns; -u; -R;
         ragged input -O<N> (and why --sam implies -k -O12).
      5  @ and @@ (before and after the computes, what each sees), the operators by kind
         with links, VALUE as a constant or :OTHER, %-decoding, multi-column handles
         (lists, regexes, ranges: every column must pass), -o and -s, @assert@ and
         @@assert@, --assert-other, --other.
      6  NAME::SPEC and NAME:=SPEC, unnamed computes, the stack language (^ : ,, the
         stack's contents written at the end), splats (a list or regex pushes several
         columns), output positions, -A<N> with computes, -i and __, maps over lists,
         regexes and ranges, renaming (P/SPEC/S, SPEC/S, P//SPEC//S) and capture group 1;
         -e (white space removed); --num-perl, --seed.
      7  the dictionary options (--kd --fd --cd --td --fasta-dict and their synonyms),
         defaults and __EXIT__, merging, map and dord and the dictionary filters (links);
         --quant-NAME with qnl and qnu (link).
      8  registers (save fetch, per row), rowno r0wno lineno, --pstore and pload with
         LIST and DEFAULT, --group and --group-first-ref with groupi groupno, pick's
         intron example.
      9  compressed input, -/PAT and -//PAT, -h -c -E<N>, --other, --assert-other,
         --demux --demux-dir --demux-buffer, compressed output files (.gz), --idx-list
         --name-list --idx-map, --progress, --threads, --version, -H/--help, -z -zz, -l,
         --list-ops-tsv.

   2. Example data, in pluk/doc/: harvest.txt and numbers.txt as far as they go; new small
      files where needed: exons.txt (genes and exons, for --group and the intron
      example), a ragged file (rows of varying length, for -O<N>), a dictionary file and
      a table file (for --fd and --td), a small fasta (for --fasta-dict).

   3. Option coverage check (agreed), in the spirit of opscheck.py: a test that every
      option in pluk --help is mentioned in the manual (and, optionally, that every
      option the manual mentions exists), so that a new option cannot go undocumented.
      At present 46 of the 57 options of --help are mentioned (most only in the section
      outlines); after sections 4-11 all should be. Added at the end of this step as a
      report; made an error when section 10 (SAM) is written.
