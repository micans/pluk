
-  SAM caching of read sequence for multiple mappings of the same read.

  Input will be assumed to be sorted by read.

  Initially we will implement read sequence caching unconditionally. In the future we may be able to infer
  that this is not necessary and then forego it.

  We will create the item or items in this step after hashing out the design first. Update step5.txt once items are defined.

  Preparation
  Achieving clarity on how read sequence caching interacts with various modes of bam/sam sorting.
  My experience (alas without examples) is that this is not well documented and potentially buggy or not well defined
  depending on the SAM producer.

  My current suggested mode of operation (this goes beyond just pick): Keep track of sequence length and 256 and 2048
  bits.  When a sequence is missing, retrieve the sequence for the last row for this read with the same bit/length match
  (or the primary alignment if no such row exists), correct for strand.  Briefly analyse whether I have missed any
  established SAM/BAM principles or conventions, either conflicting with the above or not.
  Discuss to come to an agreed plan.

  In any case, this will require maintaining a cache of rows. The cache will grow for each read with
  identical read name to the previous row. For sequence lookup I propose a simple reverse linear search.

  If the sequence cannot be retrieved, summarise what pick does at the moment.
  For pluk, I am considering different modes: skipping the row, or creating a sequence of 'N' (or potentially 'A') of
  the right length, or exiting, in all cases with a diagnostic and a counter.
  Discuss



  Outcome of the discussion (design in SAMSEQ.design.txt)

  The fill rule: a row with SEQ '*' is filled from an earlier row of the same QNAME group and the
  same segment whose stored piece of the read contains the piece the row needs; the piece follows
  from the hard clips, the strand and the full read length L. Paired-end reads are supported by
  matching on the segment (0x40/0x80); only an ambiguous segment leads to a skip. The cache is
  updated from every data row before any filtering (differs from pick). A missing sequence is
  filled with N by default.

  Testing the fill end to end needs a SAM operator. In this step the fill logic is written and
  unit tested but not yet called; wiring, the mode option, diagnostics and counters are deferred
  to the first SAM operator step (deferred.txt).
