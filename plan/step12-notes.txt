Item 1: NA and NaN; /ISNUM on nan

  Files: NUMERIC.design.txt (section 9), filter.rs (Filter::predicate), opt.rs (help).

  /ISNUM now holds for numbers other than NaN (and /NOTNUM for NaN): a NaN from parsing
  (nan, NaN) is not a usable number. inf and -inf remain numbers. The parser is unchanged:
  nan still reads as a float for arithmetic and comparisons. Rationale in
  NUMERIC.design.txt section 9 (guards against missing values; pandas, numpy and R write
  NaN for missing).

  Tests: filter-pred and filter-pred-multi (the nan row moves from /ISNUM to /NOTNUM),
  help refs; the unit test in filter.rs adds NaN and -inf.
