
- Filter synonyms; the test setup

  A small step (plan/remaining.txt C a and b).


  Outcome of the discussion

  Decisions:
   0. ~notin~ is a synonym of ~isnotin~, ~notval~ of ~hasnotval~ (plan/sketch: 'aliases to
      be consistent'). They are accepted wherever the long names are: @ and @@ filters,
      multi-column handles, and test (~notin~ with a constant dictionary as ~isnotin~;
      ~notval~ refused in test, as ~hasnotval~). -l lists them on their own lines, as
      'synonym of ..', in the filter category. pick has neither: a pluk addition, listed
      in guidelines.txt.
   1. A filter written @x~notin~D or @x~notval~D~v was until now an error ('unrecognised
      operator': any ~letters~ is read as an operator name), so no existing filter changes
      meaning. (Corrected in item 1; the draft assumed the regex filter ~.)
   2. The test setup: the user's questions, discussed and answered in step22-notes.txt;
      changes to test/Makefile or the test conventions that come out of it are made in
      item 2 [to be filled in from the questions].
