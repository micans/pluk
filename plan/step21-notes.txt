
Notes for plan/step21.txt (bio operators).


Planning

  The operator list was drawn from pick's -l (its bio tag and the sequence operators filed
  under string and format) and the experimental _strpair. Agreed in discussion: rc with
  IUPAC codes, U as T in dnatl, dnagc over A C G T (and U) only, fastq keeps its Z
  placeholder and fastQ uses it for q '*', other fastQ length mismatches NA (counted),
  the name strpair. The overview of NA introduction during SAM processing was put in
  deferred.txt.


Item 1: src/bio.rs, rc forward todna

  bio.rs (new): complement and reverse_complement, moved from samseq.rs (the step5 table:
  IUPAC codes, case kept, U to A), and todna. samseq.rs now imports reverse_complement
  from bio.rs, so the fill and rc use one table; its unit test moved along and was
  extended (U, S W, the other IUPAC pairs, gaps, the empty string).

  ops.rs: Category::Bio (list.rs: 'bio' after 'sam' in the -l order). rc and todna share
  bio_unary (NA gives NA, otherwise the bytes of the operand). The truth test of ifelse
  is now a function, truth(), used by ifelse and forward (step15 decision 2), so the two
  cannot drift apart.

  Checked against pick: rc and todna on DNA, RNA, lower case, IUPAC codes, gaps and other
  bytes: identical apart from the IUPAC complements (decided); forward with B from a
  column, 0, the empty string, a word, -1: identical; B 0.0 differs (pluk: zero, false;
  Perl: the string "0.0" is true). On qry3mm.sam and qry3vs.sam,
  isforward,qry_seq,forward: identical; rc,rc of every qry_seq gives the sequence back.

  A test pitfall met on the way: a test invocation without an input redirect (pluk -k ...
  -l rc) waits for stdin and hangs make; every invocation in test/Makefile must read a
  file or /dev/null. Also: a computed column holding NA is read by later computes as the
  text NA; the NA tests therefore produce NA inside one compute (^x,sqrt).

  Tests: bio-transform (rc todna forward on constants and columns, NA operands within a
  compute, isforward,qry_seq,forward on both SAM files, rc,rc identity, -l bio). Unit tests
  in bio.rs (revcomp, dna). list-ops and sam-rbt-edge refs (the category list).


Item 2: dnatl dnagc

  bio.rs: translate(seq, frame) and gc_counts(seq). The genetic code is the 64-letter
  string of NCBI table 1 in the order T C A G (index 16 i + 4 j + k), which is pick's
  table written compactly; a unit test translates all 64 codons and compares with the
  string. Bases are upper-cased and U is read as T before the codons are looked up; the
  {..} remainder shows them in that form (pick: upper-cased only).

  ops.rs: dnatl pops f and x (f a non-negative integer, else NA); dnagc pops N and x and
  writes 100 * GC / (A C G T U) with num::write_fixed and the digit limit of pct
  (MAX_DIGITS), so the rounding and --num-perl behaviour are those of pct.

  Checked against pick: dnatl in frames 0..3 and dnagc on the ref3.fa sequences, and both
  on every qry_seq of qry3mm.sam: identical. Synthetic sequences: identical apart from the
  decided differences (U in dnatl; N, X and gaps not in the dnagc denominator; NA for a
  sequence without A C G T U, where pick gives 0.00).

  Tests: bio-stats (synthetic sequences in several frames and digits, operand errors and
  NA operands, the ref3.fa sequences, the SAM query sequences with forward). Unit tests
  translation, gc. list-ops and bio-transform refs.


Item 3: fasta fastq fastQ strpair

  bio.rs: fasta, fastq(id, seq, Option<qual>) (None and q '*' on a sequence that is not one
  byte long give the 'Z' placeholder; a q of another length gives None), strpair. fastq
  and fastQ share the one function, so q '*' gives exactly the fastq record.

  ops.rs: fasta, fastq, strpair share bio_binary (NA operands give NA); fastQ pops three.
  Its NA for a quality of another length is counted in Machine.n_quality_mismatch and
  reported by main at the end (a note!, silenced by -q), next to the SAM report. It is a
  count of values, not rows (fastQ is normally used once per row).

  Checked against pick: fasta, fastq and strpair (pick: _strpair) on constants and columns:
  identical; fastQ identical apart from the decided cases (q '*' on a sequence of more than
  one base: Z; another length: NA). On qry3mm.sam (all QUAL '*'), 1,qry_seq,qry_qual,fastQ
  gives exactly the output of 1,qry_seq,fastq, while pick writes '*' as the quality line.

  Tests: bio-records (the four operators on records with qualities, '*' on one and on
  several bases, a wrong length, an empty sequence, the count and -q, NA operands, fastq
  and fastQ on qry3mm.sam, strpair of aln_ref and aln_qry with patchiness, -l bio). Unit
  test records. list-ops and bio-transform refs.
