Item 1: the reference stage and the parts

  sam.rs: View now has a lifetime ('d, the dictionaries, as Machine<'d>) and holds the
  seqlen and SAMFA dictionaries as references, found once when the machine is created;
  counts() no longer takes the dictionaries. The reference stage is the value of RNAME in
  SAMFA, a slice into the dictionary (no copy), cached per row as Option<Result<&[u8], Na>>.
  It asks the count stage first, so unmapped records and invalid CIGARs or POS are never
  looked up.

  The parts stage stores nothing: ref_part and qry_part take the cut points from the count
  stage (ref_trail5p_N, ref_matched_N; qry_trail5p_N, qry_matched_N) and cut the reference
  or the query sequence. The query sequence is SEQ, or the filled one (Query::Filled); its
  length is checked against qry_len (NA 'SEQ or QUAL length differs from the CIGAR'). A
  matched part that ends past the reference is NA ('CIGAR past the reference end').

  A Rust point: the step17 counts() handled the error case first because returning a
  borrow from one match arm while noting (mutating) in the other is refused. The stages
  are now split into count_stage (computes, no noting; its match only reads) and the public
  functions, which note the reason of an Err after the borrow has ended. ref_part and
  qry_part return owned bytes (Vec<u8>), which the operator pushes.

  Two new NA reasons: 'RNAME not in the reference sequences (SAMFA)' (reached only with a
  not-found value other than __EXIT__, which --sam/FILE cannot set: reserved, untested) and
  'CIGAR past the reference end'. With __EXIT__ the operator calls Machine::fail, as map
  does: 'RNAME [c9] not found in dictionary [SAMFA] (__EXIT__) at line N'.

  The reference is looked up only when an operator needs it: with only qry_ parts an RNAME
  missing from SAMFA does not end the run (pick looks it up whenever a sequence is needed).

  The lists of operators that need the reference or the query are in sam.rs
  (needs_reference, needs_query); compute::needs_query uses the latter, and compile checks
  the former against the presence of SAMFA (error naming the operator and --sam/FILE).

  Checked against pick: all six parts on qry3mm.sam and qry3vs.sam (mapped records,
  @@2/none/4), with and without --sam-nonf: identical (329 and 309 rows), including the
  filled secondaries. Synthetic records: identical where the behaviour is the same (soft
  and hard clips, I, D, exterior D and I with and without normal form, reverse strand,
  lower case); the differences are the decided ones (NA for unmapped, POS not a number, a
  CIGAR past the reference end where pick gives partial substrings, a SEQ that does not fit
  the CIGAR where pick computes the reference parts too, as pluk does, but discards the row).

  Tests: sam-parts (md5 of the six parts on both files with and without --sam-nonf, the
  first rows), sam-parts-edge (the synthetic records with and without --sam-nonf, -q,
  --sam-h/FILE with an @SQ line, qry_ parts without a fasta, RNAME not in SAMFA with a
  reference part (exit) and with only query parts (no exit), the compile-time errors).
  list-ops ref updated.


Item 2: the alignment strings, aln_all aln_allq aln_all_pack, the three options

  sam.rs: draw(reference, seq, qual, ops, pos) is a free function (unit-tested) that walks
  the CIGAR as pick's sam_compute_aln and returns the five strings (struct Aln). Each
  operation first computes its end on the reference and on the query (if it consumes
  them) and checks it against the sequence length, which gives the NA 'CIGAR past the
  reference end' (also for D and N, where pick does not check). The query end cannot be
  passed when the query stage has done its check, but draw checks it as well (SeqLength).
  mark_ends replaces the leading and trailing runs (~ to _, else - to `), ruler writes the
  position numbers; both follow pick's regular expressions and substr assignment, cut at
  the length of aln_ref.

  View::aln is the stage: computed once per row from the count stage, the reference stage
  and the query (SEQ and QUAL, or the filled ones), and kept (Option<Result<Aln, Na>>).
  QUAL '*' becomes '!' (or '.' with --sam-qlt-sim) for every base; a QUAL of another
  length than SEQ is NA (SeqLength; the fill normally catches it). The markers of
  --sam-aln-mrk are appended after the ruler is made; they need ref_trail3p_N, which is
  known whenever SAMFA is (--sam/FILE fills seqlen from the fasta), else NA (RefLength).

  The options are in sam::AlnOptions, held by the view and set by main.rs after the machine
  is created. --sam-aln-prefix=STR takes STR as given (no %-decoding, as pick; shell quoting
  covers spaces: '--sam-aln-prefix=++ '). The three options require --sam or --sam-h, as
  --sam-nonf and --sam-seqmiss.

  qry_qual now goes through View::qry_qual, which applies --sam-qlt-sim (a QUAL '*' stays
  '*'); qry_seq keeps its own small function.

  aln_lines in ops.rs joins any selection of the five strings with a separator and the
  prefix (aln_all aln_allq aln_all_pack); the single strings use it without either.

  Checked against pick: all eight operators on qry3mm.sam and qry3vs.sam (mapped
  records), plain, --sam-nonf, --sam-aln-mrk, and --sam-qlt-sim with --sam-aln-prefix,
  also combined: identical. qry_qual with --sam-qlt-sim differs on QUAL '*' as decided
  (pick gives ',' when another sequence operator is present, '' when qry_qual is the only
  one: survey 8.3). Synthetic records (N, =, X, H, P, lower case, U, exterior I and D with
  --sam-nonf, a real QUAL, past the reference end, unmapped): identical apart from the
  decided differences (X drawn as x, P not stopping the alignment, NA past the end and for
  unmapped records, which pick discards).

  Tests: sam-aln (md5 of the eight operators on both files in four option settings, the
  first record with markers and prefix), sam-aln-edge (the synthetic records with and
  without --sam-nonf and with --sam-aln-mrk --sam-qlt-sim, aln_all_pack through od, the
  prefix, qry_qual with --sam-qlt-sim, the compile-time error, the option errors). Unit
  tests: alignment_strings, alignment_ruler, quality_simplified. help and list-ops refs.
