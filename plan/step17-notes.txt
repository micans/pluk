Item 2: normal form, --sam-nonf

  src/sam.rs (new; the SAM view follows in item 3): sam::normal_form(pos, cigar) applies
  pick's four rules on the bytes of the first and last operation (no full CIGAR parse:
  the rules only look at the ends) and returns the new POS and CIGAR, or None when nothing
  changes, so unchanged rows cost two small scans. POS that is not a number: unchanged.

  Row::replace_field(i, value) splices the new value into the line and shifts the offsets of
  the later fields (padding fields at the end included). No re-split is needed, so the -O
  limit plays no role, and whole-line output (RowWriter.whole_line) writes the new record.
  POS (column 4) is replaced before CIGAR (column 6); the shift keeps CIGAR's offsets right.

  Called in Stages::judge after the @@ stage and before the computes (SAMVIEW.design.txt
  section 2), for SAM input unless --sam-nonf, on every record with at least six fields.
  The first version skipped unmapped records (FLAG 0x4, RNAME '*'), as written in the
  design in item 1; pick rewrites them too, and the design was corrected to follow pick
  (decision 3: pick's behaviour). A CIGAR '*' never changes.

  One deliberate difference: a rule that would leave nothing (CIGAR 5D: the leading-D rule)
  is not applied; pick writes an empty CIGAR and increases POS.

  Checked against pick: --sam -A on all four pick/align SAM files (qry3vs.sam has 149
  leading D and 80 leading I) is identical; --sam-nonf leaves the records as read; synthetic
  records (both rules at both ends, D twice, behind S and H, I-only, unmapped by FLAG and by
  RNAME, CIGAR '*') identical apart from 5D; @@ filters see the input POS and @ filters the
  rewritten one, and --other rows match.

  Test data: qry3vs.sam.gz and qry3mm.sam.gz (pick/align, compressed), ref3.fa (for the
  following items). Tests: sam-nf-align (QNAME POS CIGAR of qry3vs and the start of qry3mm),
  sam-nf (the synthetic records with and without --sam-nonf, @@ and @ filters, --other, -c,
  --sam-h, --sam-nonf without --sam). Unit tests in sam.rs (normal_form_rules) and row.rs
  (replace_field). Help refs.
