Item 2: capture group 1 only in the renaming shapes

  colspec.rs: expand and expand_bases share a private select(.., with_groups). expand
  (output selection, filters, plain maps) no longer looks at capture groups at all, so a
  group that does not take part (+(x)?num\d) is no longer an error outside renaming;
  before, expand went through expand_bases and refused it everywhere. expand_bases is
  unchanged and still refuses an empty or non-participating group, now only for renaming
  computes.

  compute.rs: the per-column loop asks for groups only when the compute has a Rename
  (P/SPEC/S, SPEC/S, P/SPEC/, /SPEC/, P//SPEC//S). The empty Rename that stood in for a
  group-named column without delimiters is gone: without delimiters the target is the
  column's own name, i.e. the plain map with its -i rule. /+(fib)/ (group equal to the
  name) stays the in-place case of a renaming compute and needs -i.

  Tests: o.rename-group reworked: the group cases moved to slashed forms; new cases for
  -i +num(\d) (in place), -i +(num|fib)\d (alternation), +(x)?num\d as output columns
  and as an -i map; +num(\d)::.. without -i is now the "already exists" error. Unit test
  colspec::capture_groups. Manual section 6 (paragraph and example now /+(k)g/), 14.3
  (new entry), guidelines.txt and the help text.
