[defer] Syntax/support for joining columns from a table dictionary. Somehwat invasive.

--jdict-T^[alias1=]col1[/def1],[alias2=]col2[/def2],[alias3=]col3[/def3]=foo.txt --input-join-column=key
would, before any filtering, tag col1 col2 col3 onto each row based on lookup of the key in column key.
